# InterviewIQ 🚀
> AI-Powered Placement & Career Preparation Platform

InterviewIQ helps college students land their first job faster by combining four placement tools in one platform — resume reviewer, job-description tailor, AI mock interviewer, and application tracker.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📄 **Resume Reviewer** — AI scores your resume section by section with actionable suggestions
- 🎯 **JD Tailor** — Match your resume to any job description with ATS score
- 🤖 **AI Mock Interviewer** — Practice role specific interviews and get instant feedback
- 📊 **Skill Gap Analyzer** — Find missing skills and get free learning resources
- 📋 **Application Tracker** — Kanban board to track all your job applications
- 📅 **Daily Prep Planner** — Personalized daily practice plan
